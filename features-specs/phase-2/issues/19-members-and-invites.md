# 19 Workspace members, invites and the role column

Type: grilling
Status: needs-triage
Blocked by: -

CLAUDE.md records why there are no roles yet: a workspace has one member.
Invites are when a role earns its place, because a member who should not read
or rotate provider credentials or mint gateway keys becomes possible.
Questions for the user: which roles (owner and member, or more), what each
may do, how invites are sent (by mail, which R16 makes load-bearing) and
accepted, and whether one user may belong to several workspaces, which
changes what a session is created against.
